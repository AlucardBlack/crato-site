Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 15, 2026

Crato is an independent, unofficial Android app for browsing and managing your Discogs record collection and wantlist. It is not affiliated with, sponsored by, or endorsed by Discogs or Zink Media, LLC.

Data accessed

When you sign in, Crato uses Discogs OAuth to access your collection, wantlist, ratings, custom fields, and your profile name and avatar. This data is stored only on your device — Crato has no server of its own and does not collect or transmit it anywhere except back to Discogs when you make a change.

Your Discogs sign-in

Your OAuth token is stored encrypted on your device and is never sent anywhere other than api.discogs.com.

Camera

Camera access is used only to scan barcodes for record lookup. No photos or video are stored or uploaded.

Third-party services

  • api.discogs.com / www.discogs.com — your collection, wantlist, and rating data.
  • api.deezer.com and itunes.apple.com — unauthenticated lookups for 30-second track previews.
  • Google ML Kit (on-device barcode scanning model) — may send anonymous usage telemetry to Google.
  • Firebase Crashlytics — crash reports including device model and OS version. No account or collection data is included.

No ads, no analytics, no selling data

Crato shows no ads, runs no analytics or advertising SDKs, and does not sell or share your data with anyone.

Deleting your data

Logging out wipes Crato's local copy of your data from the device. Uninstalling the app removes everything Crato stored. To revoke Crato's access to your Discogs account entirely, visit discogs.com/settings/applications.

Children

Crato is not directed at children under 13.

Changes to this policy

Updates will be posted on this page with a new "last updated" date.
