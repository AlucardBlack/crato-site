Crato for Discogs

Crato is an offline-first Android app for browsing and managing your Discogs record collection and wantlist: ratings, custom fields, value analytics, barcode scanning, and an in-store "Crate Mode" for checking have/want status and price against your collection while you shop. It only ever writes to your own collection, wantlist, and ratings.

Crato is an independent, unofficial client and is not affiliated with, sponsored by, or endorsed by Discogs or Zink Media, LLC.
